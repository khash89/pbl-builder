# Prompt 2 — Generate project ideas

Paste your learner profile in, then this.

---

Here is my learner profile. Give me **five** Project Based Learning project ideas
built outward from it — not five projects about the subject, five projects about
this person.

For each one, keep it short:

- **Driving question** — open-ended, in language this learner would use
- **What they'd make**, and **who specifically would see it** (real people beyond
  our household, named as concretely as you can from what I've told you)
- **Which of my standards it genuinely serves** — and be honest if it only
  partly does
- **Which interest of theirs it runs through** — name the actual interest, don't
  say "connects to their interests"
- **One reason it might not work** for this learner in particular

Make them genuinely different from each other. I'd rather reject four than
choose between five variations.

Constraints to respect:

- Only the ways of making things I said they're drawn to
- Buildable in the time, budget, tech, and transport I described
- If their audience comfort is low, keep the public audience and tell me how to
  build up to it — don't remove it

Then tell me which one **you'd** pick and why, and which one is the riskiest.

Two things not to do: don't invent a standards code, and don't invent a local
organisation you don't know exists. If a project needs a partner, say what kind
of partner and let me find out whether we have one.
