# Prompt 5 — Build the week-by-week calendar

Paste your milestones, then this.

---

Turn these milestones into a week-by-week calendar for [N] weeks at [N] hours a
week.

For each week: a table of what happens each session, and how I'll know each
thing is finished.

## Things I want you to do deliberately

- **Put the critique session and the revision block on different days.** If
  revision gets folded into "finish the product," it disappears.
- **Leave slack.** Tell me explicitly where the give is. First projects always
  run long, and a calendar with no give turns every slip into a failure.
- **Put the dull-but-necessary things early** — records requests, contacting
  people, anything with a lead time. Not when they're needed.
- **Schedule rehearsals as separate sessions**, with content feedback first and
  delivery feedback later.
- If my learner's audience comfort is low, **put each rung of the comfort ramp on
  the calendar as its own event.**

## Then tell me

- **Which week will overrun**, and what to take the time from. (Not the critique
  cycle — take it from Build Knowledge.)
- **What I should cut first** if we're behind, and what I must not cut.
- **The one session that matters most**, and why.

Don't pad it. If the project fits in fewer sessions than I've got, say so and use
the extra as slack rather than inventing activities.
