## Profile updates — do this now

The learner profile is out of date in specific, useful ways. **This is the step
that makes the next project better, and the one almost everyone skips.**

- [ ] `pbl_experience` — moved?
- [ ] `success_skill_target.current_level` — did the dimension move? Evidence?
- [ ] `standards_targets[].status` — which are secure now?
- [ ] `preferences.audience_comfort` — often shifts after a real audience
- [ ] `preferences.choice_appetite` — did they take more choice than offered?
- [ ] **`interests`** — new ones surface during projects. They're the next
      project's topic.
- [ ] `needs` — which scaffolds faded successfully?

**Also:** paste the Plus/Delta into the plan's `## Notes & Adaptations` section.
In three months you won't remember why week two went badly.
