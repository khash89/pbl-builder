HOW TO USE THIS

Design roles as DELEGATOR roles, not DOER roles.

  Delegator: project manager, creative director, lead researcher
             — they decide something
  Doer:      artist, materials monitor, note taker
             — they do a task

Doer roles isolate work and let a learner opt out of the thinking. Delegator
roles require judgment and are what real work looks like.

The third column is the important one: what does this person DECIDE? If the
answer is nothing, it's not a role.

Rotate across projects so nobody is permanently the note taker.
