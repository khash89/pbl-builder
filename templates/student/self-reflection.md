<!--
HOW TO USE THIS

Two days later, not the same night. On the night the learner is too flooded to
say anything real and you'll get "good" and "fine".

DO NOT GRADE OR CORRECT THIS. A graded reflection becomes a performance and you
lose the only honest channel you had. Respond, affirm, ask one question, stop.

Reconstructing the steps is itself the exercise — don't help with the first
question.

"I used to think… now I think" about how they work is often the real outcome of
the project. Watch for it.

The last question needs to be asked seriously, and then not defended.

ONE LEARNER: do it walking, or driving, or doing dishes. Sitting across a table
asking a child to evaluate a project you designed together produces performed
answers.
