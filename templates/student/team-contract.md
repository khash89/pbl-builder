HOW TO USE THIS

The learner writes the agreements, not you. Ones they wrote get followed;

BEFORE there's a disagreement. Deciding it during one doesn't work.

ONE LEARNER: this becomes an agreement between learner and adult, and it should

breakable is what makes it a real agreement rather than a list of rules.
