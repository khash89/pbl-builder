<!--
HOW TO USE THIS

Short and frequent beats long and rare. Five minutes at the end of a work
session.

The planned-vs-actual gap is the most useful line here and the reason the time
estimate is on it. Time blindness improves with feedback and almost never
improves without it — two weeks of this changes estimates measurably. No
consequence for being wrong; the data is the point.

The worry question is where you find out about problems while they're still
small. Ask it in writing, because some learners won't say it out loud.
