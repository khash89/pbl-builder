<!--
HOW TO USE THIS

Four questions. A longer form gets fewer responses and less thought in each.

Question 3 is the most useful and the most often left out. Three people confused
by the same thing is real information.

The learner should design and revise this before the event — it's a small public
product in its own right, and it has to work.

ONE LEARNER: with a small audience, ask them to sign it if they're willing.
"The woman who monitors the creek said my method was sound" is specific praise a
learner carries for a long time.

Fill one in yourself too, honestly, as an audience member. It separates your
assessor role from your audience role, and they read it differently.
