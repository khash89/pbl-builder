HOW TO USE THIS

Pick ONE question. Research 15-20 minutes. Stop and fill in a row. Say out loud
what you found and what it opened up. Then go again.

The last column is the important one. If you never have a new question, you're
collecting rather than thinking.

The sources list isn't busywork — it's what lets you defend a number when
someone asks where it came from.

ONE LEARNER: the share-out step needs a person. Ask them something you don't
know the answer to. If writing is a barrier, record it as a voice memo instead —
the thinking is the target, the handwriting isn't.
