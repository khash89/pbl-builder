# Presentation Day Checklist

**Date:** ____________  **Time:** ____________  **Where:** ______________

## The week before

- [ ] Audience invited and they've confirmed
- [ ] They know when, where, and how long
- [ ] They know what their role is (and have the feedback form)
- [ ] Rehearsed at least twice
- [ ] Anything that needs printing is printed

## The day before

- [ ] Product finished and packed
- [ ] Tech tested **in the actual room**
- [ ] Feedback forms printed
- [ ] Notes or cue card ready (a card, not a script)
- [ ] Know how long you have, and have practised inside it

## On the day, before they arrive

- [ ] Room set up
- [ ] Product in place
- [ ] Tech working
- [ ] Water
- [ ] Timekeeping sorted
- [ ] Something to do with your hands if that helps

## Afterwards

- [ ] Feedback forms collected
- [ ] Everyone thanked
- [ ] Photo, if you want one
- [ ] Something good, regardless of how it went
