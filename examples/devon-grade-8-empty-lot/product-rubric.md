## The proposal and the three minutes

| Criterion | From | Not yet | Getting there | Meets it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Names the body with actual power, and what it can't do** — verified in the minutes, not assumed | D2.Civ.6.6-8 | Names "the council" with no detail | Names the right body; powers described loosely | Names the body, cites the minute establishing its power, and states what it cannot do |
| **2. All three options costed on the same basis, assumptions written down** | 7.RP.A.3 | One option costed, or three costed differently | Three costed; some assumptions implicit | Per square foot and per year for all three, with every assumption written beside its number |
| **3. States the strongest opposing case in terms its holders would accept** | D4.7.6-8 | Opposing case is a straw man | Fair but generic | A resident holding that view read it and agreed it was their position |
| **4. Admits what the recommendation costs, and who pays it** | D4.7.6-8 | Presents it as costless | Mentions a cost | Names the cost, names who bears it, and says why it's worth it anyway |
| **5. Lands inside three minutes, pitched at people who already have opinions** | SL.8.4 | Over time, or one register for everybody | Inside time; delivery doesn't account for the room | Inside three minutes without rushing; adjusts when interrupted rather than escalating |
| **6. Specific enough to be adopted or rejected** | D4.7.6-8 | Calls for "further discussion" | Recommends a direction | A specific action a specific body could vote on |

## Check on the criteria themselves

**Could Devon meet all nine and still not have learned the standards?**

Criterion 2 is `7.RP.A.3` directly, and it's the one he could technically satisfy
mechanically — so the assumptions clause is load-bearing. Criteria 1, 7, and 8
are `D2.Civ.6.6-8`. Criteria 3, 4, and 6 are `D4.7.6-8`, which is about assessing
capacity to act through real levers — the filed comment is the lever.

Criterion 3 is the one that matters most and is deliberately the hardest to fake:
it can only be met by a third party's confirmation.

---

## The gate

**He may not write a recommendation until criterion 3 is met for all three
stakeholders.** That's not a scoring rule, it's a sequencing rule. Drafting from
a straw man wastes the draft.
